Before launch

What this version does and what still needs integration.

Does this version download from every platform?

No. It identifies common platforms and displays metadata returned by the backend. It does not bypass platform restrictions or download social-platform videos.

Will it show 4K, 2K, 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p?

Only if an authorized integration actually reports those resolutions. A video URL alone does not reliably reveal them.

Can it handle private or protected videos?

It does not bypass login restrictions, DRM, paywalls, or other access controls. Use official download or export options for content you own or are allowed to save.
